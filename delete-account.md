# Delete Your WholeTemple Account

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open WholeTemple
2. Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**
3. Confirm deletion

This immediately and permanently removes:

- Your account (email, name)
- Journal entries, mood and gratitude notes
- Discipline tracking history and streaks
- Analytics tied to your account

Deletion cannot be undone.

## Without the app

If you're unable to access the app, email **[wholetemple25@gmail.com](mailto:wholetemple25@gmail.com)** from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll process the request within 30 days and confirm by email once it's done.

## What's kept

Nothing is retained after deletion — no backups of your personal data are kept beyond what's required by law (e.g. financial records for completed purchases, which Apple/Google/RevenueCat retain per their own policies).
