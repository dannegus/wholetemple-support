# WholeTemple Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 28, 2025  
**Last updated:** July 16, 2026  
**App:** WholeTemple (iOS & Android)  
**Developer:** Dan Negussie  
**Contact:** wholetemple25@gmail.com

---

## 1. Overview
WholeTemple is a personal wellness app. I collect and use only the data needed to provide core features (account, preferences, rituals/reflections, optional in-app tips).  
I do **not** sell your data or use third-party advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. Information Collected
- **Account Information**: you start as a guest with no account. If you choose to back up your progress, your email address, display name, and an internal user ID are collected via Google or Apple sign-in (managed by Firebase Authentication — I never see or store a password).  
- **App Content You Add**: discipline selections, completions, preferences, and similar content created in the app.  
- **Health Data (optional, on-device only)**: see Section 3 — health data never leaves your device.  
- **Purchase Information**: purchase identifiers and receipts provided by Apple or Google Play for validation. Payment details are never shared with me.  
- **Device/Usage Data (Minimal)**: technical data from Firebase/Apple/Google services (e.g., timestamps, request logs). No precise location is collected.

---

## 3. Health Data (Apple Health / Health Connect)

If you choose to connect Apple Health (iOS) or Health Connect (Android), WholeTemple reads a small set of wellness metrics — **sleep, steps, active energy, hydration, and (iOS only) mindful minutes** — with read-only access, to show them beside your daily disciplines and find patterns between your practice and your wellbeing.

Health data is handled differently from everything else in this policy:

- It is stored **only on your device** and is **never uploaded** to my servers, Firebase, or any other remote service — not even when you use cloud backup.
- It is never shared with, or sold to, any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing.
- Data obtained through Health Connect is used only to provide the in-app features described above, consistent with the [Health Connect permissions policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9888170).
- All AI processing of your data (such as the weekly reflection) happens **on your device**; nothing is sent to a cloud AI service.
- Disconnecting Apple Health / Health Connect in **Settings** immediately deletes all health data stored in the app. Uninstalling the app does the same. Your health app itself is never modified.

---

## 4. Use of Information
- To create and maintain your account.  
- To operate core features (ritual tracking, reflections, preferences).  
- To process optional tips via Apple’s in-app purchase system.  
- To secure the service, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

---

## 5. Payments
All payments are processed by **Apple In-App Purchase** (iOS) or **Google Play Billing** (Android).  
The stores provide transaction status and receipts, but I never receive or store card numbers or wallet details.

---

## 6. Data Sharing
I do not sell or rent your data. Information is shared only with:  
- **Google Firebase/Firestore** (authentication, cloud backup storage).  
- **Apple** (App Store, TestFlight, In-App Purchase processing).  
- **Google Play** (app distribution, billing).  
- **RevenueCat** (subscription entitlement management — receives purchase receipts, never health data or app content).  
These providers act on my instructions and use safeguards. **Health data is shared with no one** (see Section 3).

---

## 7. Data Retention
- Account data and app content are retained while your account is active.  
- Health data lives only on your device and is deleted when you disconnect your health app or uninstall.  
- You may request deletion at any time (see Section 10).  
- Limited records may be kept if required by law, security, or dispute resolution.

---

## 8. Data Security
Industry-standard protections are used, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) and restricted database access. No method is 100% secure, but I take reasonable steps to protect your data.

---

## 9. Children’s Privacy
WholeTemple is not directed to children under 13.  
If I learn that a child under 13 has provided personal information, I will delete it.

---

## 10. International Users
Data may be processed and stored in the United States (and other regions where Firebase operates).  
By using the app, you consent to this transfer.

---

## 11. Your Choices and Rights
- **Access/Update**: update your account info in the app.  
- **Deletion**: you can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly in the app (**Settings → Account → Delete Account**), or by contacting **wholetemple25@gmail.com**.  
- **Opt-Out**: the Tip Jar is optional; the app works fully without purchases.

---

I may update this policy as the app evolves.  
New versions will be posted with updated effective dates and, when required, in-app or email notifications.
